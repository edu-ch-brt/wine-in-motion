About the physics

The wine is simulated with interacting fluid particles, gravity, pressure, viscosity, and moving bottle walls. Liquid volume is represented by a fixed particle count. The cork keeps it contained at every angle.

This is a real-time 2D approximation, not an engineering-grade fluid model. Changing the fill level resets the liquid. Rotation is limited to keep very fast gestures stable.
